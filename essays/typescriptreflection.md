## My first time with typescript

Throughout the previous years in my life I have studied multiple programming languages, typescript was not one of them. When I first tried learning typescript, I thought it was pretty simple and easy as basics are no different and simple as any other languages. But when I kept going, there were multiple differences and no matter what language you learn there are parts where it can be complicated. Now after 2 weeks of learning typescript and going through modules and lessons, I definitely have gotten better than 2 weeks ago but I am still not confident with my understanding of it. There are much more things to learn, and I am excited for what's coming next.

## Opinion for using typescript in software engineering

I feel like typescript is definitely one of the simpler languages compared to maybe like c or c++. Due to this I think typescript can be a little restricting of freedom and what you want to do compared to other languages. If you are a programmer without complete mastery, I think typescript can be an easy language to get you started off with coding. If not, I think other languages can give you better freedom and have strong points as typescript is kind of generic in my opinion. Although it might sound like I am describing typescript to be an unideal language, I personally would use typescript due to its simplicity and useability. 

## The pros and cons of this learning style

First off, I would like to start with the pros of this learning style. I really enjoy WODS as it can be a great way to test comprehension and it can be good practices as it can contain unique problems. Having problems that can vary can help with your understanding of the language and how to think to solve a certain problem. I also like the class activities as they can be good exercises to help you practice and perfect your understanding. The cons of this learning style would be the self-independent learning with like websites or textbook as it can be difficult for some to learn by just reading. This is when professors would be perfect to help teach the students instead of just handing them problems and asking them to solve it. Another con would be the homework load as I felt like I had a lot of work to do every day especially combining them with other classes. Overall, there can be better ways to do it but there are still some good parts of this teaching style.
